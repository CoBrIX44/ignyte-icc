Entry deadline raw UTC: 10/05/2026 00:00:00 Demo date raw UTC: 10/25/2026 20:00:00  The ICC Global Hackathon is a worldwide innovation challenge designed to reimagine the future of sport through technology. This hackathon embodies the ICC's vision of using innovation and technology to unlock new possibilities. By inspiring the brightest minds from diverse industries, the program aims to not only transform cricket but also contribute to the global evolution of sport in the age of AI.

This ambitious program aims to bring together students, startups, and scale-ups across the globe to tackle some of the most exciting opportunities in sport today — from enhancing fan engagement and improving athlete performance, to driving sustainability and delivering cutting-edge digital experiences.

Importantly, the hackathon places a special emphasis on elevating women in sport, spotlighting the role of technology in creating more visibility, inclusivity, and opportunity for women athletes, fans, and technologists. To encourage diversity in the participants, all teams applying will be required to have at least one female member, and there will be a special prize awarded to the top all-female team.

While rooted in cricket, the hackathon invites participants to focus across all sports in search of transformative ideas and solutions. The hackathon will run through 2026, with a program of global outreach and regional activations designed to ensure diverse participation from every corner of the sporting world.

The journey will culminate in a flagship showcase at the Dubai AI Festival in October 2026, where the most promising teams will present their solutions on an international stage.

## Problem Statements

### 1. Sport Visibility & Engagement

- Women's sports are experiencing unprecedented growth and global interest.
- How can we build innovative tech solutions — from AI-driven storytelling to digital platforms — that amplify women athletes, teams, and tournaments while creating engaging, inclusive fan experiences worldwide?

### 2. Next-Gen Fan Experiences

- Fans today expect more than just watching the game — they want to connect, participate, and belong.
- How can technology of all kinds — whether through personalization, interactive content, community-building tools, gamification, or immersive platforms — be harnessed to design the next generation of fan experiences that excite younger audiences and grow sports communities globally?

### 3. Athlete Health, Performance & Inclusivity

- Technology is revolutionizing sports science, training, and wellness.
- How can wearables, machine learning, automation, and smart analytics be applied to help athletes — especially women — train smarter, recover faster, prevent injuries, and access equal opportunities for peak performance and inclusivity?

  ## Rules

### Submission Criteria

- Open to students, startups, and scale-ups worldwide
- Cross-functional teams (tech + business + design) will be valued
- Team must consist of at least one female contributor
- Submission deadline, finalist announcement, and presentation dates are at the sole discretion of organizers, and can be changed without notice. All such changes will be updated on the hosting page

### Tracks

- **Idea track:** Concept note, mockups, or wireframes
- **Prototype track:** Working prototype, demo video (3 min max)
- **Scale-up track:** Functional product with proof of traction/pilot readiness

Women-integrated teams must have strong representation of women founders or developers.

## Selection Criteria

- Clear and well-defined USP

- Innovative features
- Sustainability and scalability of solution
- Technical capabilities

## Judging Criteria

### 1. Innovation & Creativity – 25%

- Originality of the idea
- Creativity in solving a real challenge in sports
- Differentiation from existing solutions

### 2. Technical Feasibility & Execution – 20%

- Functionality and technical soundness of the prototype/product
- Scalability and adaptability for ICC and broader sports
- Quality of documentation, architecture, and design

### 3. Impact on Women in Sport – 20%

- Tangible benefits for women athletes, teams, and fans
- Contribution to equity, visibility, and empowerment
- Consideration of inclusivity and gender fairness in design

### 4. Value to Fans, Athletes & Sports Ecosystem – 15%

- Relevance and usefulness for fans, players, coaches, or sponsors
- Potential to enhance the fan and athlete experience across ICC events
- Broader contribution to global sport innovation

### 5. Sustainability & Inclusivity – 10%

- Environmental impact and sustainability practices
- Accessibility, affordability, and multi-language support
- Representation of women and diversity in team and product design

### 6. Presentation & Storytelling – 10%

- Clarity and persuasiveness of the pitch
- Quality of demo or prototype showcase
- Ability to communicate vision, impact, and alignment with ICC
