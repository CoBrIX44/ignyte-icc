# PitchUp design contract

The primary user is an adult woman playing grassroots cricket. Her first job is recording a session; her second is understanding her week and choosing what to share. The initial experience is an explicitly labelled fictional demo, not a claimed pilot.

Use a warm off-white canvas, forest-green navigation, restrained lime accents, strong editorial headings, and cricket-ground line illustrations drawn for this product. Avoid decorative gradients and stock dashboard filler. The hierarchy is greeting, record action, weekly overview, personal activity, and a compact club rail. Activity measurements remain separate; no invented readiness score.

Desktop uses a fixed navigation rail and fluid content; mobile uses a compact header and bottom navigation. Dialogs must fit small screens and keep keyboard focus. Buttons have observable actions. Cover loading, retry, empty results, validation errors, saved feedback, confirmation for deletion, and denied-access states.

Acceptance: log a session, persist across reload, inspect factual weekly totals, review a plan comparison, explicitly publish a milestone, inspect another perspective without private records in the response, revoke publication, adjust goals, export own records, and use core flows at 390px width without horizontal overflow. No real athlete data, credentials, autonomous health advice, or claims of partner adoption.
